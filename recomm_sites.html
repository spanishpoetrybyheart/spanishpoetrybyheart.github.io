Curated Resources

Websites & Archives

A curated selection of digital repositories, audiovisual archives, and literary portals

RTVE Play Archivo

The historical audiovisual archive of Spain's public broadcaster. It houses legendary literary interviews, author profiles, poetry recitals, and cultural television programs from the past several decades.

Visit RTVE Archivo ↗

Canal March (Fundación Juan March)

An exceptional multimedia platform containing full-length video and audio recordings of scholarly conferences, poetry series, literary lectures, and musical events delivered by leading figures in the humanities.

Watch & Listen on Canal March ↗

Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes

The leading digital library of Hispanic literature, granting open access to digitized original editions, complete works, author portals, critical studies, and voice recordings of classical and modern Hispanic poets.

Browse Cervantes Virtual ↗

Poesi.as

A classic, reference anthology of Spanish and Latin American poetry. Features an extensive collection of poems arranged chronologically by author and century, from the Middle Ages to the 20th century.

Visit Poesi.as ↗
